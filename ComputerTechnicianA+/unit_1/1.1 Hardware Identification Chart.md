### 1.1 Hardware Identification Chart

**READ THIS BEFORE MOVING ON:**  These will require that the machine is completely unplugged and that you are wearing an ESD wrist strap. 

**AND READ THIS:**  After you power off your machine and physically disconnect the power cable, hold the power button down for at least 5 seconds to drain as much extra power as possible.  Be aware that there still may be power in the system, even when unplugged and 'drained' so use proper safety precautions.  The power supply is especially prone to having residual power and there may still be enough power to cause significant harm to you.

| JPG and PNG | Two common compression types for photos. The PNG is capable of having an alpha channel (see below for details).  When you take a photo with a phone or digital camera, the original image file is called a RAW file, because it is the original (“raw”) uncompressed data. These files in a modern mirrorless camera can be 40 to 60 MB per image.  To save space, cameras and phones compress the RAW files down approximately 10 to a 100 times smaller, so it can more easily be transferred.    ![][image2]![][image3]                                     JPG                        PNG |
| Alpha Channel | A clear layer on a photo. Think of it like a glass layer. It’s there, but you can’t see it. It’s represented visually as a checkerboard. It’s useful when you copy and paste images into a presentation or document; it won’t carry a background with it.   ![Alpha Channel](https://github.com/CRyan-EPIC/Classes/blob/main/ComputerTechnicianA+/unit_1/img/alpha_channel.png) |
